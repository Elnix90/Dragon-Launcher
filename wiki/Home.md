# Dragon Launcher Wiki

Welcome to the Dragon Launcher wiki. Dragon Launcher is a highly customizable, gesture-based Android launcher focused on speed and efficiency.

Your apps are launched by dragging toward points placed on the home screen. Everything can be customized, and all data stays on your device with no internet access.

Use the sidebar to browse the guides.
